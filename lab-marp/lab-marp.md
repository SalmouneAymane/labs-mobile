## 2. Need

### Initial hypothesis — not validated

Some listeners may want a more personal way to find music and keep their favorites organized.

**Question to explore:** What would make a music platform feel useful and different?

---

## 3. Analysis

### Possible user needs

- Discover music without a complicated experience.
- Save tracks and group them into playlists.
- Find artists and their music in one place.

*These are ideas to validate, not confirmed user requirements.*

---

## 4. Design

### A first model to discuss

**Listener → Playlist → Track ← Artist**

Listeners could create playlists from tracks; artists could be associated with their tracks.
